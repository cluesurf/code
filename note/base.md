# What goes in a base

A base is a **vibe**: a generalized experience, neither a verb nor a noun.
See [`vibe.md`](vibe.md) for what that means and why English cannot write
one down.

It is also a concept that is **not built out of other concepts**. Everything
else is a compound, and compounds are not specified yet.

## The test

**If it decomposes obviously, it is not a base.**

```
bird       a base
blackbird  not a base, it is black plus bird
kitten     not a base, it is young plus cat
mare       not a base, it is female plus horse
prince     not a base, it is male plus royal child
```

The bar is immediacy. A word you have to think hard about keeps its own base.
If you can invent three different breakdowns for something and none of them
is clearly right, that is not a compound, that is a base.

`command` is a base. You could argue it into "force talk" or "strong ask" or
a dozen other things, and the fact that there are a dozen is the proof that
none of them is the decomposition.

## What this rules out

**Inflections.** `going` is `go`. `dogs` is `dog`. Grammar is Tune's job and
never gets a base.

**Derivations.** `painter` is `paint` plus the agent kind. `happiness` is
`happy` plus the state kind. Anything that is a base plus a kind is a
compound.

**Obvious compounds, whatever their spelling.** `sandfly` and `mare` are the
same case. One spells its parts out and the other does not.

## What this rules in, and is easy to forget

A list built from a dictionary and a frequency table produces a good
inventory of physical objects and a thin one of everything else. The thin
half is not less real:

```
emotions, and the shades between them
states of mind, attention, absorption, dread
the spiritual and religious vocabulary of every tradition
what a body feels from the inside
social relations, obligation, standing, shame, face
the vocabulary of craft: what a maker notices that nobody else does
```

Most of these are hard to reach from a corpus, because a word for an inner
state is said less often than a word for a chair without being less needed.

## The fields

```
code    the four letters
name    one word, lowercase, unique across the whole list
layer   vibe, class, or kind
group   which shelf it sits on
note    one line saying what it is
```

**`layer` is not a part of speech**, and there is deliberately no column for
one. A vibe has no part of speech. That is the point.

```
vibe    a generalized experience, the ordinary case
class   turns a vibe into a part of speech, adding no meaning
kind    what a chain ends with, adding meaning
```

**`name` is unique and the checker enforces it.** Two codes for one meaning
is the quiet failure. Nothing breaks. From then on there are simply two ways
to say the same thing and every consumer has to know both.

**`name` is one word.** A name that needs two words is either a compound or a
concept that has not been named yet.

## Where the first 147 came from

The ontology and the 132 kinds, both carried over from the Tune work in
Tune, where they were already settled and checked. Nothing
was invented for Code.

That is the intended pattern. **A base earns its slot somewhere else first**,
and Code is where it gets a permanent number, not where it gets decided.
