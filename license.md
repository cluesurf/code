## Why these two

A permissive licence on the code means anybody can build a reader, a
converter or an editor without asking.

A citation requirement on the data means the list can spread while staying
traceable to one place. That matters more here than it would for ordinary
data: if two incompatible versions of the base list circulate with no way to
tell them apart, every consumer of either one silently breaks. Attribution is
the cheapest available defence against that.
